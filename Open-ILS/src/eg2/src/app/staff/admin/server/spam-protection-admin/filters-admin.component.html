Training data

Which training data should I use?

Using your own local data is recommended if you have at least 100 rows in the actor.usr and staging.spam_user_stage tables. Using your own local data will keep the filter responsive to your unique spam challenges. Regardless of your choice, all training data is private to your database server (not shared with a 3rd party).

Measurements

How do I configure measurements?

Measurements are the calculations that the spam filter uses to determine if something is spam or not. For each measurement specified here, the spam filter will run the specified regular expression against the configured field and count the matches. It then compares an incoming patron registration with existing patterns of spam and non-spam (the training data) based on these counts.

If your spam filter is already quite accurate, removing measurements with low importance can speed up the filter. If your spam filter is not very accurate, try editing or deleting the most important measurements; they may be swaying your results in the wrong direction.
